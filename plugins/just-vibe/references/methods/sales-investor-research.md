# Lead, competitive and investor research

Use when: lead intelligence, competitive analysis, investor deck, investor outreach, social ranking.

Research public business facts and prepare reviewable materials without inferring contact authority.

## Inspect first

- Target customer/investor thesis and geographic scope
- Primary company/product/pricing sources and dates
- Existing CRM/account context, consent and contact destination

## Method

1. Define fit criteria tied to the product and requested audience. Separate verified facts, inferred fit and unknowns in the output.
2. Compare competitors on a consistent feature/customer/job basis, using current primary evidence rather than vague rankings.
3. For investor materials, reconcile traction and financial numbers against supplied records; label projections with assumptions and sensitivity.
4. Draft personalized outreach only from supported context. Do not infer private traits, invent relationships, buy leads or send messages without explicit authority.

## Failure cases

- An old funding/pricing fact is presented as current.
- A forecast becomes a factual revenue claim.
- A connection-ranking heuristic is treated as a trustworthy measure of a person.

## Verification

- Link each consequential claim to its source/date.
- Reconcile totals and assumptions across deck, memo and spreadsheet.
- Confirm the exact account, recipients and content before an authorized send.

## Worked scenario

A lead can be marked “inferred fit: uses the relevant stack” while budget and purchasing authority remain unknown.
