# USPTO and patent landscape research

Use when: uspto, patent search, prior art.

Prepare a source-linked technical landscape, distinguishing research from a legal patentability opinion.

## Inspect first

- Technical features, jurisdiction and relevant dates
- Publication/application numbers, priority and family members
- Claim language, status and prosecution records

## Method

1. Translate the technical question into synonyms and classification/search terms; preserve the query and date.
2. Deduplicate patent families while retaining priority/publication dates and jurisdiction-specific claims.
3. Map relevant claim elements to technical features with exact source locations. Separate what a document discloses from whether its claims are valid or enforceable.
4. Report closest references and unresolved coverage; legal conclusions require appropriate professional review and current records.

## Failure cases

- A publication date is confused with priority date.
- An expired family member is treated as the status of every jurisdiction.
- A title match is presented as proof that a claim covers the product.

## Verification

- Resolve publication and application IDs on authoritative records.
- Check family, priority and current status.
- Retain a claim-feature evidence table with uncertainty.

## Worked scenario

Two patents describing similar systems may have materially different independent claims; compare the claims, not only abstracts.
