# Content, SEO and cross-platform publishing

Use when: seo, content campaign, crosspost, article writing, social publishing.

Create evidence-supported drafts tailored to audience, channel and an explicitly requested publication scope.

## Inspect first

- Audience, voice, goal and source material
- Search intent, canonical URLs and existing analytics
- Account identity, platform rules, assets and publication authority

## Method

1. Build the content around a specific reader need and verifiable claims. Keep unsupported claims as research questions, not confident copy.
2. For SEO, inspect crawlability, canonical/robots rules, structured data, headings and actual page experience before adding keywords.
3. Adapt each channel’s draft for format and context; preserve attribution and asset rights. Preview links, truncation and accessibility text.
4. Prepare an exact publish set with account, destinations, text/assets and timing. Send or publish only when that operation was explicitly authorized.

## Failure cases

- A crosspost duplicates unsupported claims or private source details.
- A robots/canonical error hides the page regardless of copy quality.
- The agent chooses the wrong account or treats draft approval as authority for every channel.

## Verification

- Verify factual claims and source dates.
- Inspect actual rendered pages/previews and links.
- Report publication IDs only from real completed operations.

## Worked scenario

A product announcement has separate website, email and social drafts; approving the website does not silently authorize mailing a list.
